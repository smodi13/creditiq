# Investment case — AMC Networks (AMCX): 10.50% Senior Secured Notes due 2032

> Independent research and underwriting exercise. The purchase price, hold period and every scenario are **hypothetical analyst assumptions**. This is not a real transaction, not a market quote and not an investment made by Fortress or anyone else. All figures are produced by the audited CreditIQ model (`models/CreditIQ_AMCX_Model.xlsx`, web route `/companies/amcx`).

## 1. Executive summary
**Question:** would a lender be adequately paid to own the first-lien 10.50% notes of a linear-TV network whose adjusted operating income (AOI) fell 46% in H1-2026, at an assumed price of 95, given that the company has no revolver and no maintenance covenants?

**Answer from the model: HOLD.** The base case clears the 12.0% hurdle (12.6% on annual periods, 12.9% on semiannual coupon timing), but the probability-weighted return is **6.1%** (6.3% on contractual timing), the downside recovery is **60.6%** against a 70% minimum, the severe case loses money (IRR −10.8%, recovery 34%), and six high-priority diligence items are open. The deciding fact is thin collateral coverage: the $1.3B of secured notes is covered about 1.1x by going-concern value at 4.5x LTM AOI and only about 0.6x at the downside trough.

## 2. Company and instrument
**Actual, from SEC filings (10-K FY2024 and FY2025, 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-06-30):**
- Owner of AMC, BBC America, IFC, SundanceTV, WE tv and the AMC+/Acorn/Shudder streaming services. Revenue $2,311.8M (FY2025), $2,421.3M (FY2024), $2,711.9M (FY2023); AOI $411.9M (FY2025), $562.6M, $670.1M; LTM Jun-26 AOI $313.0M (FY2025 + H1-26 $115.0M − H1-25 $213.9M); cash $464.0M at 2026-06-30.
- **Debt at 2026-06-30 (10-Q Note 9, principal):** 10.50% Senior Secured Notes due 15 Jul 2032 **$1,315.098M**; 4.25% Senior Notes due 15 Feb 2029 $276.706M; 4.25% Convertible Senior Notes due 15 Feb 2029 $143.750M; **total $1,735.554M** (carrying value net of $68.8M discount and $5.7M deferred financing cost: $1,661.0M — the model uses principal).
- **2026 debt events reflected:** (i) February–March 2026 exchange: $861.3M of the $875M 10.25% 2029 secured notes tendered into **additional** 10.50% 2032 notes (issued $884M early on 13 Mar plus ~$31.1M on 25 Mar; the $53.8M of extra principal is the early-tender/exchange premium), the remaining ~$13.7M of 2029 notes were redeemed on 6 Apr at 105.125%; those 2029 secured notes are therefore **extinguished and not carried in the model** (checked: 400.0 original + 884 + 31.1 = 1,315.1); (ii) 23 Feb 2026 consent solicitation amending the 2032 indenture (restricted payments up to $50M; trademark transfers limited to non-exclusive licences; narrower investments in unrestricted subsidiaries); (iii) **12 May 2026: Term Loan A ($80.0M) repaid and the $175M revolver terminated** — the credit-agreement maintenance covenants (max total net leverage 5.50x, min interest coverage 1.50x) therefore no longer apply (shown in the model for reference, not tested).
- **Terms of the notes:** 10.500% fixed, interest paid semiannually 15 Jan / 15 Jul; first-priority lien on substantially all assets of AMC Networks and the guarantors; callable from 15 Jul 2028 at 105.250%, 102.625% from 2029, par from 2030.

**Hypothetical (analyst):** $100M face at a price of 95 (cash cost $95.0M, no fees), held three years; exit/recovery assumptions per scenario; scenario probabilities 45% / 35% / 20%; hypothetical maintenance covenants (net leverage ≤5.25x→5.0x, coverage ≥1.4x→1.5x, liquidity ≥$200M) because the real ones are gone.
The 10.50% **coupon is not the return**: yield to maturity at 95 is 11.7%, base IRR 12.6%, and the expected return is 6.1% because of the downside and severe outcomes.

## 3. Thesis
Why it could be attractive: a 10.5% coupon on a first-lien instrument bought at a discount; liquidity of $464M (more than the $420M of unsecured/convertible notes due Feb 2029 that rank behind the secured notes); streaming price increases and cost action could stabilise AOI; call protection to mid-2028 limits price upside risk for the issuer's refinancing.

## 6. Base case (45%)
Coupons of $10.5/yr, exit at par after three years (exit price = MIN(100, valuation coverage at 5.5x AOI) = 100). Flows per $100 face: −95.0, +10.5, +10.5, +110.5 → **IRR 12.60% annual / 12.95% contractual, MOIC 1.38x**. Entry price at which the base IRR equals the 12% hurdle: 96.4.

## 7. Downside case (35%)
Operating assumptions deteriorate as in §5; the issuer does not default but trough leverage reaches 7.3x and coverage 1.2x, the hypothetical covenants breach from 2028, and refinancing of the 2029 notes fails the market-access test (they are, however, repaid from cash in the model's default path). Exit at **81.3** (valuation coverage of the secured notes at 5.0x AOI after cash use is below the 95 cap): flows −95.0, +10.5, +10.5, +91.8 → **IRR 6.5% / 6.7% contractual, MOIC 1.19x**. **Severe (20%)**: payment default in 2029; recovery 34.1%; IRR −10.8%, MOIC 0.70x.

The largest value drivers are the distressed multiple (each 1.0x ≈ $163M of downside EV, ~12 pts of recovery) and the 30% liquidation factor applied to a book asset base dominated by program rights. The 30% is an analyst assumption.

## 9. Risks
1. **Thin collateral coverage** — recovery 61% / 34% in downside / severe; recovery needed for MOIC 1.0x after a default is 63.5% of face.
2. **Operating decline** — AOI −46% in H1-2026; every scenario assumes continued erosion (base −4.5% revenue); base-case AOI margin 14% vs 13.9% LTM is not conservative if erosion accelerates.
3. **No revolver and no maintenance covenants** — liquidity is cash only; lenders have no early-warning trigger.
4. **Priming / leakage** — the Feb-2026 indenture amendment widens restricted-payment capacity ($50M) and permits investments in unrestricted subsidiaries within baskets.
5. **Quality of cash flow** — program-rights timing flatters FCF; $471M of programming and lease obligations would be general claims.

## 10. Protections
First-priority lien on substantially all assets; only $420M of debt (unsecured/convertible) ranks behind; call protection to 2028; $464M cash; the secured notes mature after the 2029 maturities; incurrence covenants and guarantees from the restricted subsidiaries.

## 11. Recommendation — HOLD (rule output, not a forced result)
The rule needs 0 core failures for PROCEED. Core tests: base IRR 12.6% vs ≥12.0% **pass**; expected-cash-flow IRR 6.1% vs ≥12.0% **fail**; downside IRR 6.5% vs ≥0% pass; downside recovery 60.6% vs ≥70% **fail**; base-case refinancing pass. Two core failures (<3, and base IRR passes) → HOLD. Secondary: yield 11.7% vs ≥10% pass; leverage 4.1x vs ≤6.0x pass; covenant headroom 21% vs ≥15% pass; **principal protected in severe case fails** (34 vs 95); **high-priority diligence fails** (6 open).
What would change the view: a purchase price near **82** would lift the expected-cash-flow IRR to the 12% hurdle; recovery above 70% in the downside requires a distressed multiple of about 5.3x (vs 4.5x assumed); confirmation that the content-spend run-rate keeps FCF above $100M; indenture protections against drop-downs.

## What drives base vs downside vs probability-weighted
- Coupon cash is the same ($31.5M over three years) in all three; the difference is **terminal value**: 100 (base), 81.3 (downside, coverage-capped), 34.9 recovery proceeds after a default and a one-year lag (severe).
- The probability-weighted IRR (6.1%) is the IRR of the 45/35/20 weighted cash flows (−95, 10.5, 10.5, 83.9, 7.0). It is **not** the 5.8% simple average of scenario IRRs (kept as a memo): IRR is non-linear.

## 12. Key limitations
Hypothetical price/hold; annual operating forecast (coupons re-timed to semiannual in the contractual-timing IRR, principal on annual dates, no accrued interest); recovery value uses the higher of going-concern and liquidation (generous convention); book values stand in for appraised collateral; 30% liquidation factor and distressed multiples are judgment; scenario probabilities are not calibrated; convertible treated as straight debt; no tax, hedging or funding cost at the investor level; indentures were not reviewed clause by clause (diligence items d1, d4 open); post-2026-06-30 events are not reflected.
